Why an apostille gets refused in the UAE

An apostille only works between countries in the Hague Apostille Convention. The UAE has never joined it. A New Zealand document with an apostille, including an e-apostille, has no standing with UAE immigration, ministries or employers. It has to go through document authentication and then the two UAE attestations.

This is one of the most common and costly mistakes we see. People pay for an apostille, post it to Dubai, and find out weeks later it has to be done again.

Three stages, strict order. Each office checks the stamp before it. DIA authenticates the New Zealand signature, the UAE Embassy attests the DIA authentication, and MOFA attests the Embassy's stamp. Skip one, and everything after it is wasted. We run all three in sequence and track every stage.

Degrees: the document that causes most delays

UAE employers, especially schools and universities, nearly always ask for an attested degree. A New Zealand degree certificate can't always go straight to DIA. Depending on the university and the document, it may need a Notary Public first, or a letter from the university. We check yours before anything is lodged, so it isn't rejected halfway through the chain.

Some employers also ask for the transcript, or for an equivalency certificate issued in the UAE. Tell us what your employer listed and we prepare the New Zealand side to match.

Mistakes that cost weeks

  • Getting an apostille or e-apostille instead of authentication
  • Sending the wedding-day certificate instead of a BDM marriage certificate
  • A laminated certificate. It can't be attested
  • Lodging a degree that needed a notarised copy first
  • Names on the degree, passport and visa paperwork that don't match

Frequently asked questions

Does the UAE accept an apostille from New Zealand?

No. The UAE is not part of the Apostille Convention. NZ documents need DIA authentication, then UAE Embassy attestation and UAE MOFA attestation.

What are the steps for UAE attestation from New Zealand?

Three stages, in order: DIA authentication in New Zealand, attestation by the UAE Embassy, then attestation by the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs. We handle all three.

How long does UAE attestation from New Zealand take?

Allow about 3–5 weeks from when we have your document, or 2–3 weeks with our urgent service. Ordering a new certificate or a notary appointment adds a few days at the start.

I already live in the UAE. Can you still help?

Yes. We order NZ certificates, request your police certificate and act on your signed authority, so you don't need to be in New Zealand.

Do I need to send my documents to the UAE for MOFA attestation?

No. We lodge the UAE Embassy and MOFA stages for you through the Embassy's official attestation channel.

Which documents do UAE employers usually ask for?

Most often an attested degree certificate. For family visas, attested marriage and children's birth certificates. Your employer or sponsor sets the final list.

How much does UAE attestation cost?

We quote all in, including GST, the DIA fee and the UAE Embassy and MOFA fees. Family sets are quoted together, before anything starts.

Can you order a new NZ certificate for UAE attestation?

Yes. We are an approved BDM Online organisation, so we order the right NZ birth or marriage certificate and take it through all three stages.
