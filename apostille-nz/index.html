Why documents get sent back

DIA doesn't fix mistakes or call you to sort them out. It returns the application and you start again. The most common reasons:

  • The wrong version of the document was sent
  • The document was certified by the wrong person
  • The certificate is older than the overseas office allows
  • An apostille was requested for a country that needs authentication
  • Paper or e-apostille chosen when the office wants the other
  • A translation wasn't done in a way DIA accepts
Each one costs weeks. If your visa, job, marriage or property deal overseas has a deadline, a returned application can mean missing it. Catching these before anything is lodged is the main part of what we do.

Paper apostille or e-apostille?

A paper apostille is a certificate attached to your document with a ribbon and embossed seal. An e-apostille is a secure PDF sent by email. The overseas office decides which one it accepts, and many still want paper. We confirm the right format before lodging, so you only apply once. See Paper vs E-Apostille.

How long does an apostille take in New Zealand?

DIA's standard service takes about 3 weeks. Urgent processing, for eligible documents, takes about 1 week. A new certificate or notary appointment adds some time before lodging, and courier time comes on top. Tell us your deadline when you ask for a quote and we'll tell you straight away whether it's achievable.

Apostille or authentication? It depends on the country

Many common destinations accept an apostille, including the United Kingdom, Australia, the United States, Canada, most of Europe, China, India, Japan and the Philippines. Others, such as the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Malaysia, Thailand and Egypt, need authentication instead, which is a longer process. We confirm the right route for your exact country and handle either one. See Apostille vs Authentication.

Living overseas?

You don't need to come back to New Zealand or post your originals around the world. We handle everything here and courier the finished document to you, or email an e-apostille where it's accepted. How long does an apostille take in NZ?

About 3 weeks with DIA's standard service, or about 1 week urgent for eligible documents, plus courier time. Tell us your deadline and we will tell you whether it is achievable.

Who issues apostilles in New Zealand?

Only the Department of Internal Affairs Authentication Unit. JPs, lawyers and notaries do not issue apostilles. We prepare and lodge applications with DIA on our clients' behalf.

Does my document need a notary first?

Some documents do and some do not. It depends on the document and the country. Tell us both and we will tell you, and book the notary for you if one is needed.

Can I get an e-apostille?

Yes, where the overseas office accepts one. Many still want paper, so we confirm the right format before anything is lodged.

What if my country does not accept apostilles?

Countries outside the Hague Convention, such as the UAE, Thailand and Malaysia, need authentication instead. We handle that too, from start to finish. See Document Authentication.

Can you apostille a document if I live overseas?

Yes. We handle the whole process in New Zealand and courier the document to you overseas, or email an e-apostille where it is accepted.
