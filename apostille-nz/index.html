Paper apostille or e-apostille?

Both cost NZ$37. A paper apostille is a covering certificate tied to your document with a ribbon and embossed seal. An e-apostille is a secure PDF emailed to you. Many overseas offices still want paper, so we confirm which one they accept first. See Paper vs E-Apostille.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an apostille cost in New Zealand?

DIA charges NZ$37 per apostille, paper or electronic. With our service and courier, one document returned in New Zealand is NZ$148 in total.

How long does an apostille take in NZ?

DIA processes apostilles within 15 working days. The urgent service, for an extra NZ$120, processes eligible applications within 5 working days.

Who issues apostilles in New Zealand?

Only the Department of Internal Affairs Authentication Unit. JPs, lawyers and notaries do not issue apostilles.

Does my document need a notary first?

It depends on the document. BDM certificates, criminal records and NZQA records go straight to DIA. Powers of attorney, school documents, private and medical documents must be notarised by a NZ Notary Public first.

Can I get an e-apostille?

Yes. DIA issues e-apostilles as a secure PDF by email for the same NZ$37. Check the overseas office accepts electronic apostilles first.

What if my country does not accept apostilles?

Countries outside the Hague Convention, such as the UAE, Thailand and Malaysia, need authentication instead (from NZ$74 at DIA). See Document Authentication.

Can you apostille a document if I live overseas?

Yes. We handle the whole process in New Zealand and courier the document to you overseas.
