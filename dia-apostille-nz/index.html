Quick answer

New Zealand apostilles are issued by the Department of Internal Affairs Authentication Unit in Wellington. You complete DIA's form BDM460, send it with your document by post, courier or in person (by appointment), and pay by card: NZ$37 per apostille, NZ$74 per authentication, plus NZ$120 for urgent. DIA processes within 15 working days (5 urgent) and couriers it back, from NZ$7 within New Zealand.

The DIA process, step by step

The dashed step only applies to documents DIA needs notarised first.

  1. Check the documentOriginal, notarised copy or copy, as DIA requires.
  2. Notarise, if requiredBy a NZ Notary Public, not a JP.
  3. Complete form BDM460Document details, country, apostille or e-apostille.
  4. Send and payPost, courier or in person. Card payment on the form.
  5. DIA processesWithin 15 working days, or 5 urgent.
  6. ReturnedCouriered to your address, or e-apostille by email.

What DIA needs with your document

The completed form and the document in the form DIA accepts: originals for BDM certificates and criminal records, notarised copies for powers of attorney and private documents, and so on. See the full list in NZ Apostille Requirements.

Why applications come back

  • A copy or printout sent where DIA needs the original
  • A JP or solicitor certification where DIA needs a Notary Public
  • A notarial certificate missing one of the steps DIA lists
  • Missing form pages or sections left blank
  • Apostille requested for a country that needs authentication
  • A translation arranged privately but not notarised
  • Urgent requested on an application that needs translation, which DIA does not allow

Applying from overseas

You do not need to be in New Zealand. DIA accepts applications by courier from anywhere, removes GST for overseas residents and couriers the result worldwide. The Sydney and London public offices take appointments too. The catch is getting the original document to Wellington and back, which is where most of the time goes.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the DIA apostille office?

DIA's Authentication Unit is in Wellington. Applications go by post to PO Box 10526, Wellington 6140, by courier to Level 2, 7 Waterloo Quay, Wellington 6011, or in person by appointment at DIA public offices.

Which form do I use for a DIA apostille?

DIA's form BDM460, “Request a document authentication or apostille”, available on govt.nz.

How much does DIA charge for an apostille?

NZ$37 per apostille, paper or e-apostille, plus return courier from NZ$7 within New Zealand. Urgent is NZ$120 extra and covers up to 5 documents.

How long does DIA take to apostille a document?

Within 15 working days, or 5 working days with the urgent service for eligible applications.

Can I drop my documents off in Auckland?

Yes, by appointment at DIA's Auckland public office. Book through govt.nz.

Can someone apply to DIA on my behalf?

Yes. DIA's form allows a company to apply on behalf of a client. We lodge applications this way.
