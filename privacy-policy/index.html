Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, store and share your personal information and documents, in plain English.

Effective and last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version
  • We only collect what we need to do your job.
  • We share your details only with the people and offices your job needs, such as DIA, a notary, a translator or a courier.
  • We never sell your information.
  • We delete copies of your ID and documents when we no longer need them.
  • You can ask to see or correct what we hold about you at any time.
Please check with us before sending originals. Our work can involve ID, certificates and legal records. Don't send original or very sensitive documents until we've told you the safest way to send them.

We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and its 13 Information Privacy Principles. This policy covers information we get through our website, email, phone, WhatsApp, forms, payments and the documents you send us.

1. Who we are

NZ Document Assist Limited (NZBN 9429053965790) helps people get New Zealand documents ready for use overseas. We are not a government agency, law firm, notary public, Justice of the Peace or licensed immigration adviser.

Our Privacy Officer looks after privacy questions. You can reach them at help@nzdocumentassist.co.nz.

2. What we collect

Depending on your job, we may collect:

  • your name, email, phone number and address;
  • the country your document is for and the office that will receive it;
  • details from your passport, driver licence or other ID;
  • details in the documents you ask us to help with, such as birth, marriage, death, divorce, criminal record, education, power of attorney or company documents;
  • details about another person, if you are allowed to act for them;
  • quotes, invoices and payment records;
  • messages between you and us;
  • basic website data, such as your IP address, browser and device.

3. How we collect it

Most of it comes straight from you. For example, when you contact us, fill in a form, ask for a quote, send documents or pay.

We may also get information from:

  • someone you have allowed to act for you;
  • a provider working on your job, such as a notary or translator;
  • a government office, when you ask us to get a record for you. For example, a birth, death or marriage certificate from Births, Deaths and Marriages, or a criminal record from the Ministry of Justice.

4. We only ask for ID when we need it

You can ask us a general question without sending any ID.

Some jobs do need ID. For example, when a government office needs to know who is asking for a record. In that case, we ask only for what that office needs.

5. Why we use your information

We use it to:

  • answer your questions and send quotes;
  • check who you are, when a job needs it;
  • arrange your apostille, authentication, notary, translation, courier or certificate order;
  • deal with government offices, providers and overseas offices on your behalf;
  • handle payments, invoices and records;
  • prevent fraud and misuse;
  • improve our website and service;
  • meet our legal, tax and record-keeping duties;
  • deal with any dispute or legal claim.

6. Sensitive documents

Some documents hold very personal details. Please send only what your job needs, and follow any instructions we give you for sending it.

Only the people working on your job can see your documents. For sensitive files, we may ask you to use a more secure way to send them.

7. ID numbers (Privacy Principle 13)

Your documents may include numbers such as a passport, driver licence or IRD number.

We don't create our own ID numbers to track you. We use other agencies' numbers only when your job needs them, or when the law allows. We protect these numbers like any other sensitive detail.

8. Information about other people, including children

If you give us details or documents about someone else, you must be allowed to. This could be a partner, child, family member, employee or client.

If the details are about a child, you confirm you are allowed to share them and to give us instructions. Children and young people also have their own privacy rights, including the right to see and correct their information. We take their age and interests into account.

9. Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We share it only when your job needs it, with:

  • New Zealand government offices, such as the Department of Internal Affairs and the Ministry of Justice;
  • notaries, Justices of the Peace or lawyers;
  • translators;
  • courier and postal companies;
  • our payment, accounting, email, website and IT providers;
  • overseas offices, embassies or providers your document is going to;
  • advisers, insurers, regulators, courts or police, only when the law requires or allows it.

We share only what each one needs.

10. Sending information overseas (Privacy Principle 12)

Your document is going overseas, so some of your details will too. For example, to an embassy or a foreign office.

Some of our own providers also store data overseas, such as our website form and card payment providers. Some of these are in the United States.

Before we send your information overseas, we make sure it is protected in one of the ways the Privacy Act allows. For example, the receiver has similar privacy laws, or we have a contract in place. Where we rely on your consent, we explain the risks first.

11. Keeping your information safe

We protect your information with passwords, secure devices, trusted providers and limited access.

No email, website or courier is 100% secure. If you think something you sent us has been exposed, tell us straight away.

12. Original documents

We take care of your originals while we have them. As part of your job, they may go to a notary, a translator or DIA.

Courier companies work under their own terms. Once a document is with them, they are responsible for loss or damage. We always use a tracked service.

13. How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it. For example:

  • Job records: while your job is underway, and for a reasonable time after in case you have questions or a dispute comes up.
  • Invoices and payments: for 7 years, as New Zealand tax law requires.
  • Copies of your ID and documents: deleted once we no longer need them.
  • Website and security logs: for a short time, for security and fixing problems.

After that, we securely delete or destroy it.

14. Seeing or correcting your information

You can ask to see the information we hold about you. You can also ask us to fix anything that's wrong. These rights come from the Privacy Act 2020, which also sets out the few reasons we can say no.

Email help@nzdocumentassist.co.nz. We'll check your ID before we release anything, and we'll reply within 20 working days.

15. Cookies, analytics and website logs

Our website host keeps basic logs when you visit.

We also use two tools that set cookies:

  • Google Analytics shows us how people find and use our site, such as which pages they visit.
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram) helps us measure our ads and show them to people who have visited our site.

These tools don't receive your documents, or the details you type into our forms. Google and Meta store this data overseas, including in the United States.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with Google's opt-out tool, and change your ad settings in your Facebook or Instagram account.

16. Emails and marketing

We send you messages about your enquiry or job. We only send marketing if the law allows, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

17. Privacy breaches

If something goes wrong, we act fast to stop it, find out what happened and fix it.

If a breach has caused, or could cause, serious harm, we tell you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner as soon as we can. This is what the Privacy Act requires.

18. Other websites

Our site and emails may link to government, courier or payment websites. They have their own privacy policies. We aren't responsible for how they handle your information.

19. Complaints

If you have a privacy concern, please talk to us first so we can try to fix it. Email help@nzdocumentassist.co.nz or call +64 22 100 3242.

If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

20. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, providers or the law change. The latest version and date will always be on this page.

Privacy contact

For privacy questions, or to see or correct your information, contact our Privacy Officer at help@nzdocumentassist.co.nz or +64 22 100 3242.
