ONLINE LETTER OF AUTHORITY

Authorise Us to Handle Your Documents

Complete this form to authorise NZ Document Assist to act for you. It takes about 5 minutes on your phone or computer, with no printing or scanning needed.

1 What would you like us to do?

The Ministry of Justice needs to confirm your identity. After you submit, we'll ask you for a photo of your passport or driver licence, and may send you the Ministry's own consent form to sign.

Please don't post your passport yet. Once we've confirmed your job and payment, we'll email you our address and how to send it by tracked courier.

Everyone signing must attend in person with photo ID (passport or driver licence). Please don't sign the document before the appointment. JPs don't charge for their services; our fee covers booking and coordinating the appointment.

Not sure? Choose "Not sure" and we'll confirm it for you.

Please check with the overseas authority which one they accept before choosing.

2 Your details

Please enter your details exactly as they appear on your official records.

Your details (the parent or guardian signing)

3 Where should we send the finished document?

This is where we'll courier your original passport back to you, and the paper apostille if you chose one. An e-apostille is emailed.

Couriers need a street address, not a PO box, and a phone number for the person receiving it.

4 Your authority

I authorise NZ Document Assist to act as my agent for this request only, and to:

  • apply to Births, Deaths and Marriages (Department of Internal Affairs) for the certificate(s) named above, and receive them on my behalf;
  • request my criminal record information from the Ministry of Justice and receive it on my behalf;
  • receive and hold my original passport, take it to a New Zealand notary public to certify a colour copy, and return my passport to me by tracked courier;
  • book an appointment with a notary public or Justice of the Peace for the document named above, and share the details needed for that appointment with them;
  • apply to the DIA Authentication Unit for an apostille or authentication of the document(s), using the processing option chosen, including any further New Zealand government verification the authentication requires;
  • submit the document(s) to the embassy or consulate named above for legalisation, pay its fee on my behalf (included in my estimate), and collect the document(s) from it;
  • arrange courier delivery of the finished document(s) to the address in section 3;
  • give my personal information only to the agencies and providers needed to complete these steps, such as DIA, the Ministry of Justice, a notary public or JP, the courier and the recipient named above.

This authority ends once my request has been completed, or earlier if I withdraw it in writing.

5 Sign

Type your own full name below (the same name as in section 2). This is your electronic signature and has the same effect as signing on paper.

Last step after you submit: email a clear photo of your passport or driver licence (the parent or guardian's, if the document is for your child) to help@nzdocumentassist.co.nz so we can confirm your identity. We will not start until we receive it.
