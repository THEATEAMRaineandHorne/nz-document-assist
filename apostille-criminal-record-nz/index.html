There is no NZ "police clearance certificate"

Both New Zealand Police and the Ministry of Justice confirm there is no document officially called a police clearance certificate. When an embassy, immigration office or employer asks for a police clearance, police check, penal check or criminal check, the New Zealand document that meets it is your criminal record from the Ministry of Justice.

Getting your record from the Ministry of Justice

Requesting your own record is free. You apply online at criminalrecords.govt.nz or on a paper form, with valid ID and a signed Authority to Release Information form. The Ministry aims to respond within 20 working days, and says around 90% of requests are answered in under one week.

Standard or full record? Under the Clean Slate scheme, eligible convictions are hidden on a standard record. Some countries require your full record of convictions; you get it by ticking the "full record of convictions" box on the form. We tell you which one the receiving office wants before you apply.

What DIA needs

DIA's Authentication Unit only apostilles the original paper record on Ministry of Justice letterhead (or NZTA letterhead for driving records). A printout of an emailed PDF or a copy is refused. Send us the original and we check it before lodging.

Police vetting is a different thing

Police vetting is a New Zealand Police service for approved organisations. For some visa applications Police send the vetting result directly to the overseas government, and can route it through DIA for an apostille. If you have to hand the result over yourself, you need the Ministry of Justice criminal record described above.

Mistakes we catch before they cost you weeks

  • Searching for a "police clearance certificate" that New Zealand does not issue
  • Requesting the standard record when the country needs the full record
  • Sending a printout or copy instead of the original paper record
  • Confusing police vetting with a Ministry of Justice record
  • Getting an apostille when the country needs authentication (UAE, Qatar, Thailand)
  • Leaving too little time for the Ministry, DIA and courier

Frequently asked questions

Does New Zealand issue a police clearance certificate?

No. Overseas authorities asking for a police clearance accept your criminal record from the Ministry of Justice, which is free to request.

Can a New Zealand criminal record be apostilled?

Yes. DIA apostilles the original paper record on Ministry of Justice letterhead for NZ$37.

How long does it take in total?

The Ministry usually sends the record within a week (up to 20 working days). DIA then apostilles it within 15 working days, or 5 with the urgent service.

Do I need my full record of convictions?

Some countries require it. Tick the full record box on the Ministry of Justice form. We confirm what the receiving office wants first.

How much does it cost?

The record is free. With us, apostilled and returned in New Zealand: NZ$148. Sent to the UK: NZ$194. Urgent, returned in NZ: NZ$317. All amounts include GST, government fees and courier.

Can I send a printout of the emailed record?

No. DIA requires the original paper record on Ministry of Justice letterhead.
