Quick answer

Countries outside the Hague Apostille Convention need authentication instead of an apostille. For category A countries such as the UAE, Thailand and Malaysia, DIA authenticates the document and MFAT verifies it: NZ$74, within 15 working days (5 urgent). For category B countries such as Iraq and Ethiopia, the country's embassy also legalises it: NZ$223 plus embassy fees, with no set timeframe. A birth certificate authenticated for the UAE is NZ$205 all in with us.

Which documents need a notary first?

The same rule as for an apostille. Birth, marriage and death certificates, criminal records and NZQA records go to DIA as originals. Powers of attorney, school documents, private and medical documents must be notarised by a NZ Notary Public first. A JP certification is refused. See NZ Apostille Requirements.

Translations

Many authentication countries want an Arabic, Thai or other translation. DIA can translate as part of the application for NZ$95 per document, or your own translation must be notarised. Applications needing translation cannot use the urgent service. See Translation.

Mistakes that cost weeks

  • Getting an apostille for the UAE, Thailand or Malaysia
  • Authenticating for China or Vietnam, which now take an apostille
  • Using a JP certification where DIA requires a notary
  • Planning a category B deadline around 15 working days
  • Leaving the translation until after authentication

Frequently asked questions

What is document authentication in NZ?

The process for New Zealand documents going to countries outside the Hague Apostille Convention. DIA authenticates the document, MFAT verifies it and, for category B countries, the embassy legalises it.

Does the UAE need an apostille or authentication?

Authentication. The UAE is category A on DIA's list: NZ$74, within 15 working days. With our service, a birth certificate is NZ$205 all in.

How much does authentication cost in NZ?

DIA charges NZ$74 for category A and NZ$223 for category B, plus embassy fees for category B. Our authentication service is NZ$99.

How long does authentication take?

Category A is within 15 working days, or 5 with the urgent service (+NZ$120). Category B has no set timeframe because of the embassy step.

Does China need authentication?

No. China is on DIA's apostille list, so it takes an apostille (NZ$37).

Can you authenticate documents if I live overseas?

Yes, for most documents. We order certificates, lodge them with DIA and courier the result to you overseas.
