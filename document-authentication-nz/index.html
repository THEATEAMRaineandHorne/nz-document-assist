Which countries need authentication?

Common destinations that need authentication instead of an apostille include:

RegionExamples
Middle EastUnited Arab Emirates, Qatar, Kuwait, Egypt
AsiaThailand, Malaysia, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bangladesh, Taiwan
Africa and othersNigeria, Ethiopia, Tanzania, Uganda, Iraq, Afghanistan

Some of these also need their embassy involved, which adds time and cost. The list changes too: China and Vietnam, for example, now take an apostille. We confirm the right route for your exact country before anything is lodged. See Apostille vs Authentication.

Does my document need a notary first?

Some documents do and some don't. It depends on the document and the country, and a JP certification won't do where a notary is required. Tell us what you have and we'll tell you, and book the notary if needed. See Notary + Apostille.

How long it takes

For most authentication countries, about 3 weeks, or about 1 week urgent where eligible. Countries that need an embassy step take longer and have no set timeframe, so start early. Tell us your deadline when you ask for a quote and we'll tell you straight away whether it's achievable.

Translations

Many authentication countries want an Arabic, Thai or other translation, and it has to be done in a way that's accepted, at the right stage. Applications that need a translation can't use the urgent service. We arrange the translation and fit it into the right step. See Translation.

Mistakes that cost weeks

  • Getting an apostille for the UAE, Thailand or Malaysia
  • Authenticating for China or Vietnam, which now take an apostille
  • Using a JP certification where a notary is required
  • Planning an embassy-route deadline around the standard timeframe
  • Leaving the translation until after authentication
We catch these before anything is lodged. That's the main part of what we do, and it's what keeps your deadline intact.
