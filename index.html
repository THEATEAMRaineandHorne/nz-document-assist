Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about getting your New Zealand documents ready for overseas use.

Do I need an apostille or an authentication?

It depends on the country the document is going to. Countries in the Hague Apostille Convention accept an apostille; others need authentication, sometimes with an extra embassy step. We check what your destination requires before anything is lodged.

How long does it take?

An apostille usually takes about 3 weeks, or about 1 week with the urgent service where eligible. Allow extra time if a certificate has to be ordered first or the document needs a notary or translation. We give you a realistic timeframe with your quote.

How much does it cost?

Apostille is all in from NZ$116, government fees included. Every job gets one all-in quote before any work starts, so you know the total up front.

I do not have my original certificate. Can you help?

Yes. As an approved BDM Online organisation, we can order a new New Zealand birth, marriage or death certificate for you and then handle the apostille and delivery.

Can you help if I live overseas?

Yes. You do not need to be in New Zealand. We work by email, phone and courier, and can send your finished documents to you overseas or straight to the receiving office.

What happens to my original documents?

They travel by tracked courier at every step. When the work is done, we return them to you or send them directly to where they need to go.
