OVERSEAS DOCUMENT ASSISTANCE

One Wrong Step Can Cost You Weeks

A New Zealand document going overseas can need an apostille or authentication, a Notary Public, a translation and tracked courier, in a specific order. Miss a step or do them out of order and the document is usually sent back, paid for again and delayed again.

We check what the overseas organisation is asking for before anything is sent, then handle each step for you.

Apostille or Authentication?

It depends on the destination country. We check which one you need before anything is lodged.

Does It Need a Notary?

Many private and educational documents do. We arrange the Notary Public appointment.

Is a Translation Required?

If the destination asks for one, we arrange it with an independent translator.

What Happens to the Original?

Tracked courier at every step, returned to you or sent straight to where it needs to go.
