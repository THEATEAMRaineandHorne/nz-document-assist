How It Works

A Simple 4-Step Process

We keep the administrative side of your document process clear and organised from start to finish.

1

Tell Us What You Need

Let us know the document type, destination country and what assistance you are looking for.

2

We Confirm the Process

We explain the administrative steps, our service fee and relevant third-party or government costs.

3

We Coordinate the Steps

We help organise the document handling, appointments, applications and logistics involved.

4

Your Documents Are Returned

Once the relevant process is completed, we coordinate safe return or onward delivery of your documents.

Important: NZ Document Assist provides independent administrative document assistance and coordination services only. We are not a government agency, law firm, notary public or licensed immigration adviser. Apostilles, authentications, notarial services and translations are issued or performed by the relevant authorities or appropriately qualified third parties.
