OVERSEAS DOCUMENT ASSISTANCE

Overseas Documents Can Be Confusing. We’re Here to Make It Easier.

Preparing a New Zealand document for overseas use can involve more than you expect. Depending on the document, destination country and requirements of the receiving organisation, you may come across apostilles, authentication, notarisation, translations, original-document requirements and courier arrangements.

You don’t need to work all of this out on your own. Tell us what document you have, where it is going and what you have been asked to provide. NZ Document Assist can help check the requirements, identify the likely administrative steps and coordinate the agreed process, so you can feel confident the stages we are handling have been properly organised.

Apostille or Authentication?

The correct pathway can depend on the document, destination country and what the receiving organisation requires.

Does It Need a Notary?

Some documents may need to involve an independent New Zealand Notary Public before another stage can take place.

Is a Translation Required?

If translation is required, we can help coordinate it with an appropriate independent translation provider.

What Happens to the Original?

We can help coordinate document collection, courier, tracking, return and onward delivery where appropriate.
