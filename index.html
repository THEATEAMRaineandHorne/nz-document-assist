WHEN ONE DOCUMENT BECOMES A WHOLE PROCESS

You Can Do It Yourself. The Hard Part Is Knowing What Happens Next.

At first, preparing a New Zealand document for overseas use can sound simple. Then you start finding different instructions about apostilles, authentication, notarisation, translations, original documents and couriers.

Suddenly one document can involve several different people and organisations.

Apostille or Authentication?

The first problem can be working out which process applies to your document and where it will be used.

Does It Need a Notary First?

Some documents may involve an independent Notary Public before another stage can take place, while others may not.

What About Translation?

If translation is required, you may also need to work out when it should happen and how it fits with the other document stages.

Who Gets the Original Next?

Important original documents may need to move between you, independent providers, authorities and eventually the overseas recipient.

Different Providers, Different Fees

Government charges, notary fees, translation costs and courier charges can all be separate from one another.

And Then There Is the Deadline

When documents are needed for work, study, family, business or another overseas requirement, spending days working out the process is the last thing you need.

That is the problem NZ Document Assist is designed to solve.

We help coordinate the practical administrative stages so you do not have to research every step, contact every provider and keep track of the whole process on your own.

Less Chasing. Less Guesswork. One Point of Contact.

You may be able to organise the individual stages yourself. Our service is for people who would rather have help coordinating the administrative process.

  • Tell us what document you have and where it needs to be used.
