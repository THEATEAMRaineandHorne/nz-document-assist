Companies Office records, trade mark and patent registrations, and private company papers that a NZ Notary Public has notarised. Each has its own route, so the document decides the steps and the price.

Do company documents need a notary before the apostille?

Companies Office records don't, but private company papers do. DIA accepts copies of a Companies Office extract or incorporation record as they are, while a constitution or power of attorney must be notarised by a NZ Notary Public first.

The notarial page is where these jobs go wrong. DIA expects the notary to describe what they did, sign, add their seal or stamp, give the date and place, and make it clear they saw every page. We book the Notary Public and check that page before it goes to DIA, so a set of company papers doesn't come back for one missing line.

Which document shows a NZ company is in good standing?

Start with the company extract, which shows the company's current status, addresses, directors and shareholders. If the overseas office has asked for a certificate of good standing by name, send us its exact wording and we'll confirm what will satisfy it before anything is lodged.

The Companies Register extract summarises what's on the register at that moment, including whether the company is Registered, In liquidation or Removed. If a director, shareholder or address has changed, get the register updated first, or the apostilled copy will show the old details.

Do my company documents need an apostille or authentication?

It depends on the country they're going to. Hague Convention countries take an apostille, and others need document authentication and further steps.

China takes an apostille, with a notary first where the document needs one: see NZ documents for China. Company set-up in the UAE goes through a longer route of authentication and attestation: see UAE attestation from NZ. Not sure which applies? Our guide to apostille vs authentication covers it, or just tell us the country.

Frequently asked questions

Can a NZ certificate of incorporation be apostilled?

Yes. DIA accepts a copy of a Companies Office incorporation record, so it doesn't need a notary first. We get the right copy, lodge it and deliver the apostille, from NZ$116 all in.

Can a NZ company extract be apostilled?

Yes. DIA accepts a copy of a Companies Office extract without a notary. The extract shows the company's details at the time it's produced, so check the directors and addresses are up to date before it's lodged.

Does a company constitution need a notary before the apostille?

Yes. DIA accepts a companies constitution once a NZ Notary Public has notarised the original or a copy. We book the notary, then lodge it with DIA.

Can you do several company documents at once?

Yes. Send us the whole list and we'll give you one all-in quote for the set. If it's urgent, one urgent fee can cover up to 5 documents.

Will an e-apostille work for company documents?

Only if the office receiving it accepts electronic documents, and many still want paper. We confirm the format with you before anything is lodged.

Can our lawyer or accountant arrange this with you?

Yes. They can send us the overseas office's list and we'll quote the set. We don't give legal advice, so the wording of a resolution or power of attorney stays with your lawyer.
