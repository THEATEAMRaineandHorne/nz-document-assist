You don’t need to know the rules. We do.

Ordering a NZ certificate from abroad is rarely hard because of the ordering. It’s hard because of everything around it: which version the overseas office wants, how recent it has to be, whether it needs an apostille, and getting it across the world before a deadline. Our document specialist spent 15 years in consular work, on the side that receives these documents and decides whether they’re accepted. So we already know what a visa office, citizenship registry, bank or court is going to look for.

You tell us what you need it for. We tell you exactly which certificate fits, then take care of it. That’s the whole job on your side.

How long does it take?

Certificates are usually issued within about 1 to 2 weeks. With an apostille, allow about 3 weeks in total, or about 1 week with the urgent service where eligible. International courier time is added on top. We give you an expected delivery date with your quote, so you can plan around your deadline, not guess at it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I order a New Zealand birth certificate if I live overseas?

Yes. We order the official NZ birth certificate for you as an approved BDM Online organisation and courier it to you anywhere in the world. Couriered to Australia from NZ$129 all in, or ordered, apostilled and couriered to the UK from NZ$244 all in.

Do I need a RealMe account to order from overseas?

No. Because we order through BDM Online as an approved organisation, you don’t need a RealMe account, a NZ address or a trip home. You sign our 2-minute online authority instead.

Is the certificate official?

Yes. It’s the official certificate issued by Births, Deaths and Marriages, the same legal document you would get ordering it yourself. It is not a copy or a printout.

Can you courier the certificate to any country?

Yes. We courier worldwide with tracking, to you or directly to the embassy, lawyer, registry or office that asked for it.

Does my NZ certificate need an apostille?

It depends on the country and who asked for it. Many overseas governments, courts and registries need an apostille or authentication. Tell us the country and we confirm the right one before you pay, then arrange it in the same job.

Can I order a certificate for a parent, child or estate?

Often, yes. Tell us your relationship to the person on the certificate. If anything extra is needed, we tell you before you pay.

How do I pay from overseas?

By card through our secure Stripe checkout, in New Zealand dollars. Your bank converts the amount to your currency.

What if I don’t know all the details?

Give us the best information you have. We check the record with you before ordering, so you don’t pay for a certificate that can’t be found.
