What Can We Help Coordinate?

Apostille Coordination Administrative help coordinating New Zealand documents requiring an apostille for overseas use. Document Authentication Coordination for documents requiring authentication rather than an apostille. Notary Coordination Help coordinating an appointment with an independent New Zealand Notary Public. Translation Coordination Administrative coordination with an appropriate independent translation provider. Courier & Document Return Practical coordination of dispatch, tracking, return and onward document delivery.

Do I Need to Know Exactly What Service I Need?

No. Many people contact us because they have been given instructions but are unsure how the different practical stages fit together.

Tell us what you have been asked to provide and send us any relevant instructions you have received.

The receiving organisation makes the final decision.

The overseas authority, organisation or other receiving party decides what documents and formalities it will accept. NZ Document Assist coordinates administrative processes based on the instructions and information available to us.

What If My Case Needs Several Different Steps?

That is exactly where a coordination service can be useful.

For example, one case might involve an independent notary appointment, followed by an apostille, then translation and finally international courier delivery.

Where appropriate, we can coordinate the agreed administrative workflow rather than leaving you to organise each stage independently.

Can You Help Anywhere in New Zealand?

Yes. NZ Document Assist provides nationwide administrative document coordination.

Many cases can be managed remotely using email, phone and courier. Auckland clients can also ask about in-person administrative assistance by appointment where appropriate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I send you when I first make contact?

Tell us what document you have, where it will be used, who has requested it, what you have been told is required and any deadline you have.

Do I need to send my original document immediately?

Not necessarily. Start by contacting us with information about your case. We can then explain what we need for the administrative service we are being asked to coordinate.

Will I know the cost before you start?

Yes. We provide a quote for the agreed administrative coordination service before work begins.

Can you handle the whole process?

Where the matter fits our service, we can coordinate multiple administrative stages. Any regulated professional service, government decision or independent third-party service remains the responsibility of the relevant provider or authority.

Do you guarantee that my documents will be accepted overseas?

No. The receiving organisation or authority decides whether a document meets its requirements. We help coordinate the practical administrative process based on the instructions available.

Important service information

NZ Document Assist provides administrative document assistance, coordination and logistics services only. We are not a government agency, law firm, Notary Public, Justice of the Peace service or licensed immigration adviser, and we do not provide legal or immigration advice.

Where an independent Notary Public, lawyer, translator, courier, government authority, embassy or other third-party provider is required, that provider performs and remains responsible for its own service.
