Will my birth certificate be accepted?

Often not, and that's the most common reason people come to us. Only a specific original certificate can be apostilled. Copies, scans and certified copies are refused. Many overseas offices also want a certificate issued recently, for example in the last 3 or 6 months. Send us what you have and we'll tell you straight away whether it will work, or order a new one so the job isn't sent back.

Paper apostille or e-apostille?

Both exist, and many overseas offices still insist on paper. Choose the wrong one and it's refused. We check which one the receiving office accepts before lodging. See Paper vs E-Apostille.

Frequently asked questions

Can a New Zealand birth certificate be apostilled?

Yes, as long as it is the right original certificate. Copies and scans are refused. We check yours or order a new one, then handle the apostille and delivery.

Do I need the original birth certificate?

Yes, and often a recently issued one. If you do not have a suitable original, we order a new certificate for you as an approved BDM Online organisation.

Can a JP apostille my birth certificate?

No. A JP cannot issue an apostille, and a JP certification is not accepted on a birth certificate. We handle the apostille for you.

How long does a birth certificate apostille take?

About 3 weeks, or about 1 week urgent where eligible. Allow a little extra if the certificate has to be ordered first.

How much does it cost in total?

With a suitable original you already have, from NZ$116 plus courier. Ordered, apostilled and sent to the UK, from NZ$244 all in. Urgent and other countries are quoted all in before any work starts.

Can you send it straight to the overseas office?

Yes. It can go directly to you overseas or to the receiving office, by tracked courier.

I live overseas. Can you still do it?

Yes. You do not need to be in New Zealand. We order the certificate, get it apostilled and courier it to you overseas.
