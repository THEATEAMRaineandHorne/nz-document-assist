What Does a Notary Public Do in New Zealand?

A New Zealand Notary Public is an experienced lawyer who can sign and seal documents for use overseas.

A notary can:

  • witness your signature
  • confirm a document is genuine
  • certify copies for overseas use
  • take sworn statements
  • take an oath
  • do other formal acts for documents going overseas

What the notary does for you depends on your document and what the overseas office has asked for.

Why Would I Need a Notary Public?

You usually need a notary when an overseas office, bank, lawyer or company wants extra proof that a New Zealand document or signature is real.

Powers of Attorney

Selling property or managing money overseas often needs a notarised power of attorney. See our Apostille Power of Attorney NZ guide.

Certified Copies

Some overseas offices only accept copies certified by a Notary Public, not a JP.

Company Documents

Company papers, board decisions and signatures may need a notary for deals overseas.

Property Documents

Buying, selling or owning property overseas can need notarised papers.

Affidavits & Declarations

Some overseas offices want a sworn or witnessed statement signed before a notary.

Notary Public vs Justice of the Peace

A JP and a Notary Public are not the same. You can't swap one for the other.

Which one does your document need?
Justice of the Peace Notary Public
Free to see. Witnesses and certifies documents, mostly for use in New Zealand. An experienced lawyer who signs and seals documents for use overseas.
Fine only if the overseas office says it accepts JP certification. Needed when the overseas office asks for a notary or notarised document.
Can't replace a notary when a notary is asked for. Accepted by DIA for apostilles on documents that need notarising.
If your instructions say Notary Public, notarised or notarial certificate, a JP stamp won't be enough.
For NZ apostilles:

If DIA needs your document notarised, it must be a New Zealand Notary Public. DIA says a JP or a lawyer's certified copy won't do.

What should I bring to the appointment?

We send you the notary's checklist before the day. It usually includes:

  • photo ID, such as your passport
  • your original documents, plus any copies asked for
  • the overseas office's instructions
  • any supporting papers or translations

Don't sign anything until the notary tells you to.

Does notarisation include an apostille?

No. The notary signs and seals your document. The apostille is a separate step done by DIA in Wellington. We can arrange both. See apostille help or document authentication.

Does every apostille need a notary?

No. Original NZ birth, marriage and death certificates don't need a notary. Most other documents do. See NZ apostille requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Is NZ Document Assist a Notary Public?

No. An independent New Zealand Notary Public signs and seals your document. We book the appointment and handle the steps around it.

Can I use a JP instead?

Only if the overseas office says it accepts a JP. If DIA needs your document notarised for an apostille, it must be a New Zealand Notary Public.

Do I need to visit you before the notary?

No. You go straight to the notary at the booked time. We have already checked what you need to bring.
