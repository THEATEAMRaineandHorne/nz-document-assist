What we check

These are the six things that decide whether a NZ document is accepted overseas. We check every one, so you don’t have to know what to look for.

1. The right documentThe certificate the office wants, not a printout, extract or keepsake version.
2. Recent enoughMany offices only accept documents issued in the last 6 or 12 months.
3. Names that matchYour certificate, passport and application all link up, including any name change.
4. Signed by the right personSome documents need a Notary Public. A JP certification won’t do for those.
5. Apostille or authenticationThe right one for the country it’s going to, and paper or e-apostille.
6. Nothing missingThe extra document a checklist quietly expects, like a marriage certificate to explain a surname.

Why check before you pay anyone

DIA doesn’t fix mistakes. It sends the application back, and you start again weeks later, often with another international courier and a deadline getting closer. A wrong certificate, an out-of-date one, or the wrong kind of certification are all easy to spot before anything is lodged and painful to discover after.

The check takes us minutes. Getting it wrong can cost you weeks. That’s why we offer it free to everyone, whether or not you go ahead with us. See what this looks like in practice in our real document cases.

Frequently asked questions

Is the document check really free?

Yes. We check your document and the request you were given at no cost, and you’re under no obligation to use us afterwards.

What documents can you check?

Any New Zealand document going overseas: birth, marriage, death and name change certificates, criminal record checks, degrees and transcripts, powers of attorney, court and divorce orders, and notarised or certified copies.

How quickly will I hear back?

Within 2 business hours, Monday to Saturday (Saturday until 1pm), New Zealand time.

How do I send my document for a check?

Send a photo of the document and the request you were given by WhatsApp on +64 22 100 3242, by email to help@nzdocumentassist.co.nz, or through our contact form.

Can you check documents from another country?

No. We only check New Zealand documents. A document issued by another country has to be certified or apostilled by that country.

Do you check documents for a visa or citizenship application?

Yes. We check your NZ documents against the list the visa office or registry gave you. The decision on the application is theirs, and we don’t give immigration advice.

What happens if something is wrong?

We tell you exactly what’s wrong and what’s needed. If you’d like us to fix it, we send one all-in price first and only start once you say yes.
