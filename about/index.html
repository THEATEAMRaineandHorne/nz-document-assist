ABOUT NZ DOCUMENT ASSIST

Making Overseas Document Administration Easier to Manage

NZ Document Assist helps take the administrative burden out of preparing New Zealand documents for overseas use — especially when the process involves several different steps, providers and requirements.

Published 6 September 2026 · Updated 10 September 2026

If you have ever been told that a document needs to be notarised, apostilled, authenticated or translated, you may already know how quickly a simple request can turn into a confusing process.

Our role is to help coordinate the practical administrative steps so you do not have to manage every stage by yourself.

Why Overseas Document Processes Can Be So Frustrating

One set of documents can involve several different organisations, professionals and requirements.

You may find yourself trying to work out:

  • whether you need an apostille or authentication
  • whether your document needs to be notarised first
  • whether the original document is required
  • whether a certified or translated version is needed
  • which organisation needs to receive the document next
  • how to safely send important original documents between providers
  • how long each separate stage may take
  • which fees belong to the government, notary, translator or courier
  • whether the overseas organisation will accept the finished document

And if one stage is missed or completed in the wrong order, it can mean extra emails, extra appointments, repeat courier costs or delays when the documents may already be needed urgently.

NZ Document Assist helps coordinate those practical steps so you do not have to chase every provider, organise every handover and keep track of the entire process on your own.

A Single Point of Contact for the Administrative Process

The frustrating part of overseas document preparation is often not one individual task. It is having to coordinate several separate tasks with different people and organisations.

You may need to contact a Notary Public, organise an apostille or authentication, arrange a translation, send original documents by courier and then make sure everything reaches the correct destination.

NZ Document Assist was created to provide one administrative point of contact for the practical coordination of those steps.

Instead of repeatedly working out what happens next and arranging every handover yourself, you can ask us to coordinate the agreed administrative workflow.

We Are Not Trying to Replace the Professionals

Some overseas document processes require an independent Notary Public, lawyer, translator, government authority, embassy, courier or another specialist provider.

NZ Document Assist does not replace those providers.

Our role is administrative: helping coordinate the practical process, organising agreed stages and acting as a point of contact for the work we have agreed to manage.

For example:

If your document needs to be notarised, the independent Notary Public performs the notarial act.

We can help coordinate that appointment and, where appropriate, continue helping with the next administrative stage such as an apostille, authentication, translation or document return.

Why Use a Coordination Service Instead of Doing Everything Yourself?

You can often arrange individual document services yourself.

The value of NZ Document Assist is not that every individual step is impossible to do yourself. It is that you may prefer not to spend your time researching requirements, contacting several providers, arranging document handovers, tracking original documents and trying to keep the whole process moving.

For someone dealing with an overseas move, employment, study, family matter, business requirement or another deadline, that administrative work can become a significant distraction.

Our service is designed for people who would rather have someone coordinate the practical process and keep it organised.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you part of the New Zealand Government?

No. NZ Document Assist is an independent private administrative assistance and coordination service.

Are you a Notary Public?

No. Where notarisation is required, the notarial act is performed by an independent New Zealand Notary Public.

Are you a law firm?

No. NZ Document Assist is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.

Do you provide immigration advice?

No. We are not licensed immigration advisers and do not provide immigration advice.

Can I organise these services myself?

In many cases, yes. Our service is for people who would prefer help coordinating the administrative stages rather than researching, contacting and managing each provider independently.

Can you help if I do not know whether I need an apostille or authentication?

You can start by telling us what document you have, where it will be used and what the receiving organisation has asked you to provide.
