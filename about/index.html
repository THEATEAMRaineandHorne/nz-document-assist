ABOUT NZ DOCUMENT ASSIST

Making Overseas Document Administration Easier to Manage

NZ Document Assist is an independent New Zealand administrative service created to help people organise the practical steps involved when New Zealand documents need to be prepared for use overseas.

Overseas document processes can involve several different organisations, requirements and stages. Our role is to help make the administrative side easier to understand, organise and manage.

Instead of leaving you to coordinate every practical step separately, NZ Document Assist can help manage the agreed administrative workflow and keep you updated along the way.

Independent

We are a private administrative assistance service and are not part of the New Zealand Government.

New Zealand Based

We help coordinate New Zealand documents being prepared for use in other countries.

Clear Communication

We explain the administrative work we can coordinate and provide a clear quote before agreed work begins.

Why NZ Document Assist Exists

People are often told that a document needs an apostille, authentication, notarisation, translation or another formality before it can be accepted overseas.

The difficulty is that the process can involve different authorities, independent professionals and document-handling stages.

NZ Document Assist was created to provide a single administrative point of contact for the practical coordination of those steps.

What We Can Help Coordinate

Depending on your document and the service you ask us to provide, we can assist with the administrative coordination of:

  • Apostille processing
  • Document authentication
  • Independent Notary Public appointments
  • Translation arrangements
  • Courier, tracking and document return
  • multi-step overseas document cases

How We Approach Your Documents

Clear Before Complicated

We aim to explain the administrative process in straightforward language rather than making an already unfamiliar process feel more complicated.

Transparent Fees

Our administrative service fee is kept separate from government, notary, translator, courier and other third-party charges wherever applicable.

Careful Coordination

Important documents can move through several different stages. We focus on organising the agreed administrative workflow and keeping track of what happens next.

Clear Boundaries

Where a regulated professional or government authority is required, that independent provider performs and remains responsible for its own service.

We Are Not Trying to Replace the Professionals

Some overseas document processes require an independent Notary Public, lawyer, translator, government authority, embassy, courier or another specialist provider.

NZ Document Assist does not replace those providers.

Our role is administrative: helping coordinate the practical process, organising agreed stages and acting as a point of contact for the work we have agreed to manage.

For example:

If your document needs to be notarised, the independent Notary Public performs the notarial act. We can help coordinate the appointment and, where appropriate, continue helping with the next administrative stage such as an apostille, authentication, translation or document return.

Nationwide Assistance

You do not need to be based in Auckland to use NZ Document Assist.

Many document-assistance cases can be coordinated throughout New Zealand using email, phone and courier.

Auckland clients can also ask about in-person administrative assistance by appointment where appropriate.

What You Can Expect From Us

  • a quote before agreed work begins

  • careful handling of the information and documents relevant to your case
  • no claims that we can control government or third-party processing times

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you part of the New Zealand Government?

No. NZ Document Assist is an independent private administrative assistance and coordination service.

Are you a Notary Public?

No. Where notarisation is required, the notarial act is performed by an independent New Zealand Notary Public.

Are you a law firm?

No. NZ Document Assist is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.

Do you provide immigration advice?

No. We are not licensed immigration advisers and do not provide immigration advice.

Can you help if I do not know whether I need an apostille or authentication?

You can start by telling us what document you have, where it will be used and what the receiving organisation has asked you to provide.

You can also read how our document assistance process works.
