Sending Original Documents

Some overseas offices want the original, not a scan or a copy. Check what they asked for before anything is sent. We can check this for you first, free.

Before we send your originals, we suggest you keep a clear scan for your own records. We always use a tracked service, and a signed-for service for valuable papers.

Can You Send Documents Overseas?

Yes. We can send your documents to most countries, either back to you or straight to the office that needs them. The price depends on the country and the speed of service. It is all included in your quote.

Some countries have their own customs or delivery rules. We check these when we quote.
