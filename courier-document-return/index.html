Important documents can move through several different stages before they are ready for overseas use.

They may need to travel between you, an independent Notary Public, a translation provider, an authority processing an apostille or authentication, and finally back to you or onward to another destination.

NZ Document Assist can coordinate the practical logistics around the document process we are managing for you.

What Can We Coordinate?

Depending on your case and the agreed service, we can help coordinate:

  • courier booking for document dispatch
  • document collection arrangements where available
  • tracking details and practical delivery updates
  • return delivery after an apostille or authentication process
  • movement of documents between agreed independent service providers
  • onward delivery after a notary or translation stage
  • return of completed documents to you
  • international document courier arrangements where appropriate services are available

Courier Coordination as Part of Your Document Process

Courier handling is often only one small part of a much larger process.

For example, your documents may require:

  • notary coordination
  • apostille coordination
  • document authentication
  • translation coordination

Where these services form part of the work we have agreed to coordinate, we can help manage the practical document movement between stages.

One point of contact

Instead of arranging each administrative step separately, you can ask NZ Document Assist to coordinate the agreed document workflow and related logistics in one place.

Before Sending Valuable Original Documents

If originals are required, confirm the receiving organisation's instructions before dispatch. Where possible, keep clear scans or copies for your own records and use a tracked service appropriate to the importance of the documents.

Important:

NZ Document Assist can coordinate agreed courier arrangements, but we cannot guarantee courier delivery times, customs clearance, or protection against loss or damage. Those matters are governed by the independent courier provider's terms and any relevant authorities.

Sending Original Documents

Some overseas document processes may require original documents rather than scans or ordinary copies.

If original documents need to be sent, it is particularly important to understand the instructions you have received before dispatching them.

We can coordinate agreed courier arrangements, but the organisation receiving the document determines what format, document type and delivery method it will accept.

Tracking Your Documents

Where courier tracking is available, we can help coordinate the tracking information for the document stage we are managing.

Actual transport, delivery performance, delays, customs processing and courier timeframes remain the responsibility of the independent courier provider and relevant authorities.

Can You Coordinate International Delivery?

Yes, where appropriate courier services are available, we can help coordinate international delivery of documents.

International delivery charges depend on factors such as destination, service level, parcel size and the independent courier provider selected.

Customs, import, delivery and receiving requirements can also differ between countries.
