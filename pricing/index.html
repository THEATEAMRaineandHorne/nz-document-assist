Pricing Questions

Is the quote free?

Yes. Tell us which document you need, where it will be used and any deadline. There is no charge or obligation to proceed.

What is the difference between NZ$116 and NZ$148?

The NZ$116 starting price covers an e-apostille delivered by email. The NZ$148 starting price covers apostille processing and return of your original document by courier within New Zealand. Both include the standard DIA apostille fee and GST.

Are certificate ordering and courier fees added to every package?

No. They are added only when required and not already included. The NZ$244 UK birth certificate package includes ordering and UK delivery. The NZ$148 apostille option includes NZ return courier.

Do your prices include government fees and GST?

Yes. Each option includes GST and the government fees stated in its description. Your quote lists any additional services and confirms the total before paid work begins.

How much does urgent processing add?

Urgent processing adds from NZ$169, including the government urgent fee and GST. We confirm eligibility and the expected timeframe before you proceed.

What if I need a notary or translation?

We include any required notary and translation services in your quote. These are additional to the starting apostille prices unless specifically included in your package.
