What Is Not Included in These Prices?

Our advertised prices are for NZ Document Assist's administrative assistance and coordination.

Depending on your case, separate charges may apply for services provided by others, including:

  • government processing fees
  • Notary Public fees
  • translator fees
  • embassy or consular charges
  • courier, postage and international delivery charges
  • other independent professional or third-party services

Where known and relevant, we aim to make the distinction between our fee and external charges clear in your quote before paid work begins.

Why Do Some Prices Say “From”?

A straightforward case involving one document and one administrative stage can require considerably less work than a matter involving several documents, different destination requirements or multiple independent providers.

“From” pricing shows the starting NZ Document Assist service fee while allowing us to quote fairly for the actual administrative work required.

Frequently Asked Questions About Pricing

Is the initial document assessment really free?

Yes. There is no NZ Document Assist charge for the initial assessment. We use the information you provide to understand the document, destination and likely administrative pathway. If paid assistance is appropriate, we provide a quote before you decide whether to proceed.

Do your prices include GST?

Yes. The NZ Document Assist service fees shown on this page include GST.

Are government fees included?

No, unless we expressly include them in a written quote. Government charges are separate from the NZ Document Assist service fee.

Are Notary Public fees included?

No. A Notary Public is an independent professional and charges separately for their own service.

Are translation fees included?

No, unless expressly stated in your written quote. Our Translation Coordination fee covers our administrative coordination. The independent translator's fee is separate.

Can you tell me the likely total cost before I start?

Where the required stages and external charges are reasonably clear, we will explain the expected NZ Document Assist fee and known third-party costs before paid work begins. Some external charges can vary depending on the document, provider or destination requirements.

Can you coordinate the whole process for me?

In many cases, yes. If your matter involves several administrative stages, ask us about Full-Service Document Handling. We can explain what we may be able to coordinate and provide a quote based on the work involved.

Does Priority Administration make government processing faster?

No. Priority Administration applies only to work performed directly by NZ Document Assist. We do not control the processing times of government authorities, notaries, translators, embassies, couriers or other independent providers.
