Frequently Asked Questions About Pricing

Is the initial document assessment really free?

Yes. Tell us the document, the country and what you have been asked for. We tell you what it needs and send one all-in quote. There is no charge and no obligation.

Do your prices include government fees and GST?

Yes. The prices on this page include DIA's government fees and GST. Your quote shows one all-in price, so there is nothing extra to pay to DIA yourself.

Will I know the total before work starts?

Yes. You get one all-in price for your job before any work starts, and you only pay once you have accepted it.

How does the urgent service work?

For eligible documents, we lodge with DIA's urgent service, which takes about 1 week instead of about 3, and we handle your case first. It adds from NZ$169 to your quote, including the government's urgent fee.

What if my document needs a notary or a translation?

We arrange them for you and include them in the same all-in quote, so you see one price for the whole job.

Why use you instead of applying to DIA myself?

You can apply yourself. With us, your document is checked by a specialist with 15 years' consular experience before it is lodged, we arrange any notary or new certificate, and we track it to delivery. Mistakes are caught before they cost you weeks.
