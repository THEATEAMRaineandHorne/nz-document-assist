What Is Not Included in These Prices?

Our advertised prices are for NZ Document Assist's administrative assistance and coordination.

Depending on your case, separate charges may apply for services provided by others, including:

  • government processing fees
  • Notary Public fees
  • translator fees
  • embassy or consular charges
  • courier and international delivery charges
  • other independent professional or third-party services

Where known and relevant to your case, we aim to make the distinction between our fee and external charges clear in your quote.

Why Do Some Prices Say “From”?

A straightforward case involving one document and one administrative stage can require considerably less work than a case involving several documents, different destinations or multiple providers.

Using “from” pricing allows us to show you the starting cost while still quoting fairly for the actual administrative work required.

Frequently Asked Questions About Pricing

Are government fees included?

No. Government charges are separate unless they are expressly included in your written quote.

Are Notary Public fees included?

No. A Notary Public is an independent professional and charges separately for their own service.

Are translation fees included?

Our Translation Coordination fee covers our administrative coordination. The independent translator's fee is additional unless expressly included in your written quote.

Does Priority Admin Handling make the government process faster?

No. Priority Admin Handling applies only to administrative work performed directly by NZ Document Assist. We cannot guarantee or control processing times of government authorities or other independent providers.

Can you quote a multi-step case?

Yes. If your documents require several stages, tell us what you have and where the documents will be used. We can quote the administrative coordination work based on the process involved.

Important service information

NZ Document Assist provides administrative document assistance, coordination and logistics services only. We are not a government agency, law firm, Notary Public, Justice of the Peace service or licensed immigration adviser, and we do not provide legal or immigration advice.

Independent third-party fees are separate unless expressly included in a written quote.
