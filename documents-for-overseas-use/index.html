The four terms you will see

Apostille: a government certificate that makes a NZ document valid in Hague Convention countries. Paper or electronic. See Apostille NZ.

Authentication: the longer route for countries outside the Hague Convention, sometimes with an embassy step. See Apostille vs Authentication.

Notarisation: a Notary Public certifies or witnesses the document first. A JP won't do where a notary is required. See JP vs Notary Public.

Translation: only if the overseas office asks, and it has to be done in an accepted way. See Translation.

How long it takes

About 3 weeks for most apostilles and authentications, or about 1 week urgent where eligible. Allow extra if a new certificate or a notary is needed. Countries that need an embassy step take longer. Tell us your deadline and we'll tell you straight away whether it's achievable.

Living overseas?

For most documents you don't need to be in New Zealand. We order certificates as an approved BDM Online organisation, handle every step here and courier the result to your overseas address. Documents that need a Notary Public in person are the exception; tell us your situation and we'll set out the options.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to use a New Zealand document overseas?

Usually an apostille (Hague Convention countries) or authentication (other countries). Some documents need a new certificate or a Notary Public first, and some countries also want a translation. We work out exactly what yours needs.

Can you help if I don't know what I need?

Yes. That is most of our clients. Tell us the document, the country and any instructions you have, and we confirm the route free and send one all-in quote.

How much does it cost to get a document ready for overseas?

From NZ$116 all in for an original document apostilled. A birth certificate ordered and sent to the UK is from NZ$244. Authentication is from NZ$205. Notarised documents are quoted all in.

How long does it take?

About 3 weeks for most documents, or about 1 week urgent where eligible. Allow extra time if a new certificate or a notary is needed.

Can you send the document straight overseas?

Yes. We can send it by tracked courier to you overseas or straight to the receiving office.

Do you give legal or immigration advice?

No. We handle the document process. For advice on a visa or legal matter, speak to a licensed immigration adviser or a lawyer.
