1. What NZ Document Assist does

We provide practical administrative support for New Zealand documents that may need to be prepared for use overseas. Depending on the service you request, our work may include checking the information you provide, explaining administrative steps, coordinating with third-party service providers, arranging document delivery, and keeping you updated on progress.

Our services may include coordination relating to apostilles, authentication, notary appointments, document certification, translation, courier services and return delivery. The exact work included in your service will be set out in your quote, invoice, email confirmation or other written communication from us.
