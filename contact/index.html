Confused? We Are Here To Help

What starts as a request for one document can quickly turn into several different questions and providers.

Apostille or authentication?

Get it wrong and the document is refused overseas. We confirm the right route before anything is lodged.

Does a notary come first?

Some documents need one, some don't, and a JP won't do. We tell you and book it if needed.

When does translation happen?

Done at the wrong stage, it has to be redone. We arrange it and fit it in at the right step.

Who has the original document now?

With us, it is tracked at every step and delivered to you or straight overseas.

Already started and got stuck? Send us what you have and where it is up to. We will pick it up from there and take care of the remaining steps.
