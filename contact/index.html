Not Sure What Service You Need? That Is Completely Fine.

Many people contact us because they have been given an overseas document requirement but are not sure what needs to happen next.

You may have been told that a document needs an apostille, authentication, notarisation, translation or certification — or you may simply have been told to prepare a New Zealand document for overseas use.

You do not need to understand the whole process before asking for help.

Start with what you know.

Tell us what document you have, which country it will be used in, who has requested it and what instructions you have received. If you are not sure what those instructions mean, that is okay too.

Already Tried to Work It Out Yourself?

What starts as a request for one document can quickly turn into several different questions and providers.

Apostille or authentication?

Different overseas requirements can involve different administrative processes.

Does a notary come first?

Some cases involve an independent Notary Public before another document stage can happen.

When does translation happen?

Translation may be another separate stage with its own provider and requirements.

Who has the original document now?

Original documents may need to move between several providers, authorities and destinations.

You can often organise these individual services yourself. NZ Document Assist is for people who would rather have help coordinating the practical administrative stages instead of spending their time chasing each part of the process.

Questions Before You Contact Us

What if I don't know whether I need an apostille or authentication?

That is fine. Tell us what document you have, where it will be used and what the receiving organisation has told you.

Can I send you the instructions I received?

Yes. Written instructions can be very useful in understanding what the receiving organisation is asking you to provide. Please do not send sensitive identity documents with your initial enquiry.

Do I need to be in Auckland?

No. Many cases can be coordinated throughout New Zealand using email, phone and courier.

Will I know the cost before work starts?

Yes. We explain the administrative service we can provide and give you a quote before agreed work begins. Government, notary, translation, courier and other independent third-party costs may be additional.

Can I organise these services myself?

In many cases, yes. Our service is for people who prefer help coordinating the administrative stages rather than researching, contacting and managing each provider themselves.

Important service information

NZ Document Assist provides administrative document assistance, coordination and logistics services only. We are not a government agency, law firm, Notary Public, Justice of the Peace service or licensed immigration adviser, and we do not provide legal or immigration advice.
