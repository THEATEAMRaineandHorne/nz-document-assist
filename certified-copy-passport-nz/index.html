Why a JP copy gets refused

An apostille confirms the signature and seal of the person who certified the copy, and a JP's certification isn't one that can be apostilled. So a JP copy that's fine for a New Zealand bank is refused the moment it needs to go overseas with an apostille. Many people find this out after they've paid a courier and waited weeks. We get it right first time.

Which pages, and how recent?

Some offices want only the photo page; others want the signature page or visa pages too. Many want a copy certified recently, and some require a minimum validity left on the passport. We check the recipient's instructions so the notary certifies exactly what's needed.

Live overseas?

A New Zealand Notary Public has to see your passport in person, so this is harder if you're not in New Zealand. Tell us your situation and we'll set out the options that work for you.

Frequently asked questions

Can a JP certify a copy of my passport in NZ?

Yes, for most New Zealand purposes where the organisation accepts a JP. A JP copy is not accepted for an apostille or authentication for overseas use.

How do I get my passport copy apostilled in NZ?

It has to be certified by a Notary Public first, then apostilled. We book the notary, handle the apostille and deliver it, with one all-in quote.

How much does it cost to apostille a passport copy?

Because a notary is involved, we send one all-in quote covering the notary, apostille, government fees and delivery before anything is booked.

Can you apostille the passport itself?

No. A certified copy is apostilled, not the passport. We arrange the right certified copy for you.

Do I need to send my original passport?

Usually not. With our notary route you keep your passport and only attend one appointment.

I live overseas. Can you still help?

A NZ Notary Public has to see your passport in person, so it depends on your situation. Tell us where you are and we will set out the options.
