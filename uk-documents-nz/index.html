Why doesn't a DBS check cover New Zealand?

A DBS check only looks at UK records, so your years in New Zealand don't show up. That's why a UK employer can ask for a New Zealand check as well as the DBS.

The UK government says DBS checks only provide information from UK-based data sources, and strongly recommends employers get criminal record information from the person's home country. In health and education, there are some statutory requirements for employers to carry out these checks. The employer can't get yours for them, though: GOV.UK's entry for New Zealand says prospective UK employers cannot apply.

That leaves you, on the other side of the world from the Ministry of Justice. As a Ministry of Justice registered third party, we request your NZ criminal record on your signed authority, check your ID against it first so it isn't held up, and email you the result.

Do I need a NZ police check for a UK Skilled Worker visa?

If you're applying from outside the UK for a job in education, health or social care, you might. The Home Office asks for a certificate from each country where you've spent 12 months or more in the last 10 years, or since you turned 18 if you're under 28.

Those 12 months can be added together, so several shorter stays in New Zealand can count. A partner applying with you, aged over 18, falls under the same requirement. Your sponsor tells you whether you need one when your certificate of sponsorship is issued. Most other UK visa routes don't ask for one at the moment, and neither does extending your stay from inside the UK. We don't advise on visas. Our job is getting you the right New Zealand document, on time.

When should I request it?

If New Zealand is the last country you lived in, the Home Office wants the certificate issued no earlier than 6 months before you apply, so requesting it too soon can waste it. Leaving it too late causes its own problems: here's how a late police certificate became a five-week delay. Give us your planned application date and we'll tell you when to start.

Will the emailed result be accepted?

The Home Office's guidance accepts an original or scanned copy of a criminal record certificate, so the result we email you can go into your application. It's in English, so it doesn't need translating.

Have a conviction concealed under the Clean Slate scheme?

Tell us before we lodge anything. The Ministry of Justice says the Clean Slate scheme doesn't apply outside New Zealand, so a UK office may want your full record rather than the standard one. That version comes to you directly from the Ministry when you apply for your own, so we'll tell you what to do before you pay.

How do I get a NZ police check from the UK?

Pay NZ$35, sign our two-minute online authority and email us a photo of your passport. We match your details to your ID, lodge the request with the Ministry of Justice and email you the result, usually in under a week.

Nothing is printed, posted or witnessed. The Ministry's formal standard is 20 working days, so if a start date or visa date is close, give it to us when you order. A clean record still produces a result: a letter confirming there's nothing to disclose.

Can I use my UK driving licence as ID?

No. The Ministry doesn't accept overseas driver licences. A current UK or other overseas passport works, and so does a NZ passport up to two years expired, or a NZ driver licence. If you don't have any of these, we'll tell you the alternative before you pay.

How do I get a NZ birth certificate in the UK?

We order it for you as an approved BDM Online organisation and courier it to your UK address for NZ$169 all in. You don't need a RealMe account, a NZ address or a trip home.

Lost yours? A replacement is a new certificate from the same record, and it's just as official as the first one. That matters for a British passport application, which asks for originals or replacements rather than photocopies. If a detail is uncertain, we check the record before ordering, so you don't pay for a failed search.

A NZ birth certificate is usually issued within about 1 to 2 weeks, and courier to the UK usually takes about 3 to 8 working days. You get an expected delivery date when you order. Births, Deaths and Marriages has no urgent service, so tell us your deadline before you pay and we'll say whether it's realistic.

Which NZ documents does a UK Ancestry visa need?

GOV.UK asks for your full birth certificate, plus full birth certificates for the parent and grandparent your claim is based on. If you or your parent were born in New Zealand, those NZ certificates are the ones we order.

Evidence of a name change, such as a marriage certificate, may be needed too, so a family set is often more than one document. Tell us who was born where and we'll quote the NZ certificates as one job, sent together. Your grandparent's UK certificate comes from the UK side, and whether you qualify is for the Home Office to decide.

Which NZ certificates do UK banks and solicitors ask for?

Usually a marriage certificate to prove a name change, or a death certificate when a relative's estate reaches the UK. Both have to be official certificates from Births, Deaths and Marriages.

Took your partner's name when you married in New Zealand? A NZ marriage certificate links your birth name to the name you use now. When a parent or relative dies in New Zealand, a UK solicitor, bank or pension provider may want the NZ death certificate. Several offices may each keep one, so tell us how many and we'll order them together.

Marrying in the UK after a New Zealand divorce? The register office asks for proof that your marriage has ended, which means your NZ Dissolution Order.

Do NZ documents need an apostille for the UK?

Only if the office asking says so. The Home Office's criminal record guidance, for example, asks for an original or scanned copy and doesn't mention an apostille.

When a UK solicitor, a court or a third country does want one, it has to come from New Zealand. GOV.UK is blunt about it: you cannot get documents issued outside the UK legalised through the UK service. DIA does it here, so we order the document, get the apostille and courier it to you or straight to the office. See birth certificate apostille and criminal record apostille.

Frequently asked questions

Can my UK employer request my NZ police check for me?

No. GOV.UK says prospective UK employers can't apply for it, so the request has to come from you. We make it on your signed authority and email you the result to pass on.

Is a NZ police check the same as a DBS check?

No. A DBS check covers UK records. A NZ police check is your record from the New Zealand Ministry of Justice. A UK employer can ask for both.

Is a NZ police check the same as NZ police vetting?

No. Police vetting is a New Zealand Police service that only approved organisations can use, and you can't order it yourself. The document you can send a UK employer or the Home Office is your Ministry of Justice criminal record.

I left New Zealand years ago. Can I still get my record?

Yes. Nothing needs posting. You pay, sign our online authority and email us a photo of your current passport, and the result is emailed to you in the UK.

What do I get if I have no convictions?

You still get a result: a letter from the Ministry of Justice confirming there's nothing to disclose.

How long is a NZ police check valid in the UK?

It has no expiry date of its own. If New Zealand is the last country you lived in, the Home Office wants it issued no earlier than 6 months before you apply. UK employers set their own limits.

Can I order a NZ birth certificate from the UK without RealMe?

Yes. You don't need a RealMe account, a NZ address or a trip home. We order the official certificate on your signed online authority and courier it to your UK address, tracked, for NZ$169 all in.

Can you send a NZ certificate straight to my solicitor or employer?

Yes. We courier it to you, your solicitor, your employer or the office that asked for it.

Can I pay from the UK?

Yes. You pay by card through our secure Stripe checkout. Prices are in New Zealand dollars, and the checkout usually lets you pay in pounds instead.
