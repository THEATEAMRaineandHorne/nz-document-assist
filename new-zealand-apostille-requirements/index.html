Quick answer

For a New Zealand apostille, DIA's Authentication Unit needs the document in the right form: the original for BDM certificates, criminal records and NZQA records, or a copy notarised by a NZ Notary Public for powers of attorney, school, private and medical documents. You also need DIA's BDM460 application form and the NZ$37 fee per apostille. Processing is within 15 working days, or 5 with the urgent service (+NZ$120).

What goes in the application

  • DIA's BDM460 request form, completed and signed
  • The document, in the form shown in the table above
  • Payment: NZ$37 per apostille, plus NZ$120 if urgent and NZ$95 per document if DIA arranges the translation
  • A return courier option: NZ$7 within New Zealand, NZ$26 to Australia, NZ$53 to the UK

DIA accepts applications by post, courier or in person. We prepare the form, check it against the document and lodge it for you.

Originals, copies and PDFs

A PDF or photocopy of a BDM certificate is refused: DIA needs the sealed original. If you only have a PDF from a university or employer, it usually has to be printed and notarised before DIA will apostille it. NZQA records carrying the NZQA logo are the main exception and go to DIA as issued.

Paper apostille or e-apostille?

Both cost NZ$37. A paper apostille is a covering certificate tied to your document with a ribbon and embossed seal. An e-apostille is a secure PDF emailed to you. Many overseas offices still want paper, so confirm what they accept first. See Paper vs E-Apostille.

Translation

Only needed if the overseas office asks for it. DIA can translate as part of the application for NZ$95 per document. A translation you arrange yourself must be notarised by a NZ Notary Public before DIA will accept it. Applications that need a translation cannot use the urgent service. See Translation.

Does the certificate need to be recent?

DIA does not set an age limit, but some overseas offices do, often 3 or 6 months for birth and marriage certificates. If yours is older than they allow, we order a new one through BDM Online (NZ$35 plus our NZ$15) before lodging.

Mistakes that send documents back

  • Getting a JP to certify a document DIA needs notarised
  • Sending a photocopy or scan of a BDM certificate
  • Applying for an apostille when the country needs authentication
  • Choosing an e-apostille when the office wants paper
  • Arranging your own translation without having it notarised
  • Using a certificate the overseas office says is too old

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to get an apostille in New Zealand?

The document in the form DIA accepts (original, notarised copy or copy, depending on the document), DIA's BDM460 form and the NZ$37 fee. We prepare and lodge it for you.

Can a JP-certified copy be apostilled?

No. Where DIA requires notarisation, it must be done by a NZ Notary Public. JP and ordinary solicitor certifications are refused.

Which documents need a Notary Public first?

Powers of attorney, school documents, private and medical documents, and translations you arrange yourself. BDM certificates, criminal records and NZQA records go to DIA without a notary.

How much does an apostille cost in NZ?

DIA charges NZ$37 per apostille. With our service and courier, an original document returned in New Zealand is NZ$148 in total. A notarised document is typically NZ$287 to NZ$362.

How long does an apostille take in NZ?

Within 15 working days, or 5 working days with DIA's urgent service for an extra NZ$120. Urgent is not available if a translation is needed.

Can I get an apostille if I live overseas?

Yes. We handle it in New Zealand and DIA can courier the document straight to your overseas address.
