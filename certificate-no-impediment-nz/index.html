What is a Certificate of No Impediment?

A CONI confirms that, after searches by the Registrar-General, no lawful reason has been found to stop you marrying or entering a civil union. It is issued under the Marriage Act 1955 to New Zealanders who intend to marry outside New Zealand. You apply on BDM's form BDM189 with details of yourself, your partner, the intended marriage and any previous marriages, plus copies of your passport and any divorce or death certificates.

CONI or certificate of search?

Some countries ask for a "single status certificate". BDM does not issue a document with that name. Depending on the purpose, the overseas office may accept a CONI or a certificate of search, which confirms a search of the marriage and civil union registers.

DocumentTypical purpose
Certificate of No ImpedimentGetting married or entering a civil union overseas
Certificate of searchProof of single status for visas, foreign passports or overseas registration

Always use the document the overseas authority names. We check this with you first.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Certificate of No Impediment cost in NZ?

BDM's base fee is NZ$180, plus postage. Some countries add their own charges. With an apostille and courier, returned in New Zealand, expect about NZ$328 in total.

How long does a CONI take?

Allow 14 days plus delivery for BDM. If it also needs an apostille or authentication, BDM advises allowing about 4 weeks in total.

Does a CONI need an apostille?

Only if the wedding country requires it. Hague countries take an apostille (NZ$37); others need authentication (from NZ$74).

Is a single status certificate the same as a CONI?

No. BDM does not issue a single status certificate. For marriage overseas it is usually a CONI; for visas or registration it may be a certificate of search.

Can the CONI be sent straight overseas?

Yes. After the apostille, DIA can courier it to you or the overseas registry, for example NZ$54 to most of Europe.
