Why the notary must come first

DIA's Authentication Unit lists powers of attorney among the documents that must be notarised by a New Zealand Notary Public before an apostille or authentication. A Justice of the Peace or an ordinary solicitor certification does not meet this rule. See JP vs Notary Public.

What the notary must include

For DIA to accept it, the notarial certificate must:

  • describe what the notary did, for example "witnessed a signature" or "certified a true copy"
  • include the notary's name and signature
  • carry the notary's seal or stamp
  • state the date and place of signing
  • show that every page was seen, for example by marking each page

We check the certificate against this list before lodging, so it is not sent back.

Sign it in front of the notary

If the notary is witnessing your signature, do not sign the power of attorney beforehand. Bring your passport or other photo ID and the overseas party's instructions.

We do not draft powers of attorney

The wording must suit the overseas country and transaction, so it should come from your lawyer or the overseas lawyer, bank or registry. New Zealand enduring powers of attorney have their own legal requirements. Once the document is ready, we handle the notary, DIA and delivery.

An apostille confirms the notary's signature and seal are genuine. It does not confirm the power of attorney is legally suitable overseas.

Frequently asked questions

Does a NZ power of attorney need notarising before an apostille?

Yes. DIA requires powers of attorney to be notarised by a New Zealand Notary Public before it will apostille or authenticate them.

Can a JP witness it instead?

No. Where DIA requires notarisation, a Justice of the Peace or ordinary solicitor certification is not accepted.

How much does it cost in total?

Notarised, apostilled and returned in New Zealand: typically NZ$287 to NZ$362, including the notary's own fee. Category A authentication: NZ$344 to NZ$419.

How long does it take?

The notary appointment depends on the notary's availability. DIA then takes up to 15 working days, or 5 working days with the urgent service.

Can you draft the power of attorney?

No. We do not draft documents or give legal advice. Your lawyer or the overseas party prepares it; we handle the notary, DIA and delivery.

Can it be sent straight to the overseas lawyer or bank?

Yes. DIA couriers it to the address on the application, for example NZ$54 to most of Europe.
